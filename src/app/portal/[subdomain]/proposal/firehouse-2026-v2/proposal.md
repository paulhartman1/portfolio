# Digital Transformation Proposal (v2)
## A Modern Website & Unified Digital Platform
### Firehouse Art Center

**Prepared for:** Firehouse Art Center Board of Directors  
**Prepared by:** Paul Hartman  
Common Ground Technology LLC  
July 2026 (Revised July 10, 2026)

## Executive Summary
The Firehouse Art Center has built an outstanding reputation as one of Longmont's most important cultural organizations. Its website should reflect that same level of excellence.

Today, visitors encounter multiple disconnected systems to donate, register for classes, learn about exhibitions, purchase tickets, and engage with the organization. While each system serves a purpose, together they create a fragmented experience for visitors and additional administrative work for staff.

This proposal recommends replacing the current website with a modern digital platform built specifically for the Firehouse. Rather than attempting to solve every problem at once, development is divided into manageable phases that provide immediate value while creating a foundation for future growth.

*The objective is not simply to build a better website.*  
*The objective is to create a platform that supports the Firehouse's mission for years to come.*

## The Opportunity
A visitor inspired to support the Firehouse should not have to navigate multiple websites, different payment systems, or inconsistent experiences.

Likewise, staff should not have to manage several independent tools to accomplish everyday work.

The proposed platform will provide:
- One consistent online experience
- Easier content management
- Simplified administration
- Improved accessibility
- Mobile-friendly design
- Secure online donations
- A foundation for future online services
- Ownership of your website and data

Rather than replacing systems every few years, this platform is designed to evolve alongside the organization.

## Why a Phased Approach?
Digital transformation projects are most successful when delivered incrementally.

Each phase produces a complete, usable product while reducing risk, allowing feedback, and spreading investment over time.

**The Firehouse maintains complete control over whether to continue after each phase.**

## Phase One
### Public Website Refresh Pilot (v2)
**Investment:** $2,000  
**Estimated Timeline:** Approximately 3–4 weeks following approval and receipt of required content and deposit.

### Phase One Includes
#### Public Website
A completely redesigned website that reflects the quality of the Firehouse Art Center.

Including:
- Home page
- About
- Mission
- Staff
- Board
- Volunteer information
- Contact information
- Responsive design
- Search engine optimization
- Accessibility improvements

#### Donations
Donation integration using existing systems already in operation.

Including:
- Donation page integration with current forms/workflows
- Improved donor path from public pages to donation flow
- Consistent site styling around donation entry points
- Preservation of existing donation operations in the short term

#### Events & Exhibitions
Staff-managed operational tools and public pages for:
- Current exhibitions
- Upcoming events
- Filtered event and exhibit navigation sections
- Exhibition and event details with images
- Targeted admin tools for event/exhibit updates
- Ongoing staff updates without developer involvement in these core areas

#### Artist Membership Applications
Online applications allowing artists to submit information directly through the website, with practical admin workflows for weekly onboarding.

Staff can:
- Review submissions
- Approve applications
- Add and update member profiles
- Upload member photos
- Maintain the public member gallery

#### Administration
Secure administrative tools are included for high-frequency operational workflows: Events, Exhibits, and Members. This keeps staff self-sufficient in core areas while maintaining pilot scope discipline.

#### Scope Boundaries (v2)
- Included: targeted admin tools for Events, Exhibits, and Members
- Included: public redesign, navigation changes, search icon, and content migration
- Not included: full site-wide CMS for all informational pages
- Not included: native e-commerce/shop rebuild (separate enhancement)
- Not included: full replacement of external operational systems in this pilot

#### Launch Services
- Content migration
- Testing
- Deployment
- Staff training
- Launch support

### Phase One Result
At completion, the Firehouse will have:
- A modern website
- Donation integration with current systems
- Staff-managed events and exhibitions
- Staff-managed member onboarding and gallery updates
- A focused pilot foundation for broader platform work
