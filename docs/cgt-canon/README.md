# CGT Canon

The Canon is the shared language of Common Ground Technology.

It is the current best understanding of the CGT discipline: the continuous transformation of organizational evidence into trusted organizational understanding.

This is the first implementation of the discipline. It is a living body of knowledge, not static documentation.

## What the Canon Is

The Canon is the authoritative body of definitions for how CGT understands the organizations it serves.

These definitions are the vocabulary shared between humans, AI agents, and software. When any of them use a word from the Canon, they are agreeing on a specific, traceable meaning.

The codebase is an implementation of the discipline. The Canon is the discipline itself.

## Why the Canon Exists

CGT is not a CRM. It is not an AI consulting platform.

CGT is an evidence-driven organizational inquiry system. Its software exists to continuously transform organizational evidence into trusted organizational understanding.

Software cannot do this without a shared vocabulary. The Canon is that vocabulary, recorded once so it does not have to be rediscovered in every conversation, ticket, migration, and feature.

## Why Definitions Evolve

Every definition in the Canon is a hypothesis about how organizational understanding works.

Evidence leads to observations. Observations support hypotheses. Hypotheses survive inquiry. Understanding evolves.

Definitions are expected to improve over time through inquiry and evidence. A definition that is contradicted by evidence is not a failure of the Canon. It is the Canon doing its job.

History in each document is intentionally nearly empty. It exists to record the inquiry that changes a definition, one reasoned change at a time.

## Why Software Follows the Canon

The Canon must be the source of truth for terms, boundaries, and relationships.

When software and the Canon disagree, the Canon wins. Software is a reimplementation of the discipline and is free to change. The Canon is the contract that software is held to.

If the Canon followed the software, the vocabulary would drift with every implementation detail and the shared language would fragment. The Canon precedes the software so that the software can be judged against it.

## Why Evidence Matters

Understanding that cannot be traced to evidence is opinion.

The Canon has no room for opinion. Every definition should eventually be traceable to inquiry: to the evidence gathered, the observations drawn, and the hypotheses that survived.

An assertion without evidence is a placeholder awaiting inquiry. It is marked as such and improved, never asserted as truth.

## Why AI Agents Should Read the Canon First

Every AI agent that implements a feature for CGT begins by reading the Canon.

The Canon defines the meaning of the concepts the agent will touch: what an observation is, what an engagement contains, what evidence means where it is stored, and how these relate.

An agent that builds from the Canon builds with the discipline. An agent that builds without it invents a parallel vocabulary and debts the organization's understanding.

Read the Canon before writing code.

## How to Read the Canon

Every concept document follows a consistent structure:

- **Status** — maturity of the definition
- **Definition** — what the concept is
- **Purpose** — why the concept exists
- **Characteristics** — what defines and bounds the concept
- **Produces** — what the concept generates
- **Consumes** — what the concept depends upon
- **Relationships** — how the concept connects to other concepts
- **Open Questions** — known uncertainty awaiting inquiry
- **History** — the record of how the definition evolved

## The Canon of Concepts

- [Project](project.md) — the organizational unit under inquiry
- [Engagement](engagement.md) — a bounded relationship of inquiry
- [Session](session.md) — a discrete instance of inquiry within an engagement
- [Evidence](evidence.md) — the raw material of organizational understanding
- [Artifact](artifact.md) — a durable output of inquiry
- [Observation](observation.md) — a claim drawn from evidence
- [Hypothesis](hypothesis.md) — an idea that survives inquiry before it becomes understanding
- [Journey](journey.md) — the evolving map of an organization's understanding

## Status and Maturity

- **Draft** — the definition is a scaffold awaiting inquiry and evidence

Definitions move through maturity levels only through evidence, never by fiat.

## The Future of the Canon

Today, Markdown is the canonical editable source.

CGT itself will become capable of storing, versioning, and evolving its own Canon. When that happens, these Markdown files will become generated artifacts of that system.

The structure of this directory is designed for that migration: clean, consistent, single-concept documents with explicit relationships and history. The Canon does not depend on Markdown being the source of truth. It depends on the definitions surviving the move.

## Amend the Canon Through Inquiry

To change a definition, show the evidence and the inquiry that leads to the change. Record it in History. Let the new understanding stand until better evidence arrives.

No edits by assertion. Every edit is a finding.
